PD-SVAK installer

Installs PD-SVAK on a supported Pixel from Chrome or Edge. It erases everything on the phone.

  1. 1. Check your browser

    Checking…

  2. 2. Prepare the phone

    • Back up anything you want to keep. Installing erases the phone.
    • Settings → About phone → tap Build number 7 times to turn on Developer options.
    • Settings → System → Developer options → turn on OEM unlocking. If it is greyed out, your phone is carrier-locked (common on Verizon models) and cannot be installed.
    • Use a USB-C cable plugged directly into the computer, not a hub. If something fails, try another port or cable first.
    • Turn the phone off, then hold Power + Volume down to enter the bootloader.

  3. 3. Unlock the bootloader

    Your phone will ask you to confirm. Use the volume keys to pick Unlock the bootloader, then press Power.

  4. 4. Download and verify

    Downloads the signed image for your exact model and checks its signature and SHA-256 hash before anything is written.

  5. 5. Install PD-SVAK

    Don't unplug the phone. It will restart several times; the installer reconnects by itself, and asks you to reconnect if it can't.

  6. 6. Add the PD-SVAK verified-boot key

  7. 7. Lock the bootloader

    Required. An unlocked phone lets anyone with physical access replace the OS. Confirm on the phone with the volume keys and Power.
